# 0.1.1 - next step

The corridor, both transport models and the measurement rig are in place
(see `archive/0.1.0-plan.md` and `archive/0.1.0-worklog.md`). The next step is
about the parts of the draft that are still only flavour.

## 1. the economy must bite

right now the gates cost credits and energy but a flight never fails because of
them: the reactor (2.5 energy/s) covers two open gates (1.6/s) and still leaves
room for the engines. the interesting decision the draft describes - "costly to
open, costly to keep open" - needs a budget that can actually run out.

proposal:
- the reactor only produces while the *hull* is intact: damage cuts the output
- closing a gate is free but the corridor behind it loses its beacon, so a closed
  exit gate means the ship has to hold position in the medium (expensive: hull
  wear and the mind)
- add a "jump window": the exit gate can only capture while it is open, and
  opening it costs 12% of the credits, so the player has to decide when

metric: with a fixed budget, how long does the player survive and how often do
they arrive? add a `budget` sweep next to the turbulence sweep.

## 2. two scales of reality

the corridor maps `x` units inside hyperspace to `x*k` units in the far universe.
right now `k` is only a number in the readout. make it real: a second sector with
its own gates, its own noise field and its own guide curve. the ship leaves
sector A through the exit gate and enters sector B through the paired entry
gate, at the far side of the world. the flight becomes a chain, and a failure in
sector A costs the whole trip.

proposal: sector B reuses the same sim with a second guide, a different seed and
gates placed so that the entry of B is where the exit of A lands. the "x*k"
relation becomes the placement rule instead of a number.

## 3. one more hazard layer

the draft lists "wearing hull, mentality, etc." - the mind is currently a slow
degradation of the pilot's aim. add something that moves: slow drifting
anomalies (dead zones where the medium is calm but the gas is thin, or shear
lines that tear the hull). they make the route change between flights, so a
memorised path stops working.

## 4. player telemetry

the reference pilot is not a player. record human runs the same way the trials
are recorded (success, time, hull, energy, deviation) and compare the two
distributions. that answers the question the whole project is about: how much of
the difficulty is the medium and how much is the pilot.

## keep

the layer toggles, the reference pilot, the probe of the natural path and the
sweep rig stay as they are - they are the measuring instrument and must not move
while the game moves.
